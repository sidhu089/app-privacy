Size Fix — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Size Fix: Compress Image & PDF ("the app") is an Android app published by SVT Tech. This policy explains what data the app handles and who receives it.

Summary

Your images and PDFs are processed only on your device and are never uploaded by the app. The app shows ads from Google AdMob; to show and measure those ads, Google's ad service collects some device data, described below. We (the developer) do not run a server and do not receive any of your files or personal information.

Your files

  • Images and PDFs you choose are compressed or converted on your phone.
  • Results are saved only to your device's storage, or shared only when you use your phone's share sheet.
  • Temporary files made during processing are deleted afterwards. The history list is kept on your device and can be cleared in the app or by clearing the app's data.

Permissions

  • Photos and media / files: to let you pick the images or files you want to compress, and to save the results.
  • Camera (optional): to take a photo to compress.
  • Internet and network state: used only to load ads.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show ads. When an ad is requested or shown, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version)
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate location
  • ad interactions (for example, which ads were shown or tapped) and diagnostic data

Google uses this data to deliver, personalise and measure ads and to prevent fraud. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads depending on your device).

Analytics and other third parties

Apart from Google AdMob, the app contains no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services, and we do not sell or share any data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Everything the app stores is on your device. Uninstall the app, or go to Settings → Apps → Size Fix → Storage → Clear data, to remove it. To manage data collected by Google for ads, use your device's ad settings or My Ad Center.

Changes

If the app's data handling changes, this page will be updated before the new version is released, with a new "Last updated" date.
